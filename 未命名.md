Let's consider the equation of motion:

$$
\partial_ {\mu}(F_ {\mu \nu}+H_ {\mu \nu} ) = -g \hat{n} \cdot [\vec{X}_ {\mu}\times (\hat{D}_ {\mu}\vec{X}_ {\nu}-\hat{D}_ {\nu}\vec{X}_ {\mu}) + \partial_ {\mu}(\vec{X}_ {\mu}\times \vec{X}_ {\nu}) ]
$$

and 

$$
\hat{D}_ {\mu}(\hat{D}_ {\mu}\vec{X}_ {\nu}-\hat{D}_ {\nu}\vec{X}_ {\mu}) = g(f_ {\mu \nu}+H_ {\mu \nu}+ X_ {\mu \nu})\hat{n}\times \vec{X}_ {\mu}.
$$

Now let us consider a string that connects a monopole-antimonopole pair, the vortex solution. The string streches in the z direction. Take the time-dependent ansatz for the gauge field:

$$
\hat{A} = -\frac{1}{g} \hat{n}\times d\hat{n}, \quad  \vec{X} = \frac{f(r)}{g} e^{i(kz-\omega t)} \hat{n}\times d\hat{n}
$$

where 

$$
\hat{n}=(\cos n\varphi, \sin n\varphi, 0)^{T}.
$$

Take this ansatz to the equation of motion listed at the beginning, simplify it, eventually obtain the equation of motion for $f(r)$ function. Show the detailed derivation along the process.
