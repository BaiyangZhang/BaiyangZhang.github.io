

  

As calculations in LSPT are pushed to two loops and higher orders in the coupling $g = \sqrt{\lambda}$, or extended to $3+1$ dimensions where ultraviolet renormalization in the Schrödinger picture must be carried out explicitly order by order, the number of operator contractions and multi-meson Fock space components grows rapidly. An efficient and systematic bookkeeping tool is therefore needed to organize the perturbative expansion. In this paper, building on the passive formulation of LSPT, we introduce an intuitive, easy-to-track diagrammatic method—which we call Jarah diagrams—designed specifically for Schrödinger-picture state and mass perturbation theory. By decomposing each interaction Hamiltonian $H_k$ according to the net number of mesons created or annihilated, we represent operator actions as vertices and multi-meson Fock space projections as vertical slices across which the coupling order increases from right to left. We establish complete diagrammatic rules for vertex weights, energy denominators, and composite lower-order state vertices, and apply this method to compute renormalized vacuum and one-meson wave function corrections up to $\mathcal{O}(g^3)$, demonstrating the explicit cancellation of infrared and ultraviolet divergences and providing a streamlined framework for higher-order soliton mass and wave function corrections.
